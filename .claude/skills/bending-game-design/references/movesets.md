# Move catalogue (starting point): one lead shape per move, lore-true mechanics, original names

Format: **Name** (slot) - mechanic. *Lead shape* (what the VFX is built around). Lore note where it matters.
Every move gets the house VFX rules from the `bending-vfx` skill (anime shapes, dark accent, outside haze, mass).

## WATER
Tide style: **Lashline** (Z) - a long water whip, 2-hit. *A sweeping water ribbon with a foam edge.* / **Undertow**
(X) - pull a target toward you with a grabbing stream. *A coiling stream that snaps back.* / **Breaker** (C) - a
wave that rolls forward and knocks down. *A curling wave crest.* / **Riptide Ring** (V) - a spinning water ring
around you that hits outward. *A rotating ring of water blades.* / **Ultimate: Grand Tide** (F) - raise a towering
wave and crash it. *The wall of water + its crash.*
Glacier style: **Shardfall** (rapid ice daggers), **Rime Wall** (ice wall), **Frostbind** (freeze feet / ice
prison), **Glacier Spike** (ice spikes up from the ground), **Ultimate: Whiteout** (a field of ice spires).
Mist style: **Veil** (fog cloud that hides you), **Scald** (a steam burst: lore-true as moving steam), **Drift
Step** (dissolve-dash through mist).
Sub-skills: Healing - **Mending Flow** (heal over time, glowing water on the target), **Restore** (revive at
Master). Plant - **Thornroot** (vine snare from the ground), **Swamp Colossus** (ultimate: a plant-and-water
body). Blood (Outlaw, full moon) - **Puppet Hold** (immobilise and drag a target; no visible water, the target's
body moves; VFX: dark red veins/aura + forced pose). Spirit - **Calm** (purify / cleanse debuffs), **Spirit Tide**.

## EARTH
Bedrock: **Stone Fist** (thrown boulder), **Rising Column** (pillar under a target, launches), **Bulwark** (earth
wall), **Faultline** (a crack that runs forward and erupts), **Ultimate: Mountainfall** (a ring of huge pillars
then a slam). Stonehide: **Gauntlet** (stone fists, heavy M1), **Shell** (rock armour, damage reduction),
**Quarry Grab** (sink a target to the knees). Tremor: **Groundwave** (rolling earth wave), **Sinkhole** (quicksand
zone), **Earthsurf** (movement: ride a moving ridge).
Sub-skills: Metal - **Cable Lash** (metal cable whip / grapple), **Plate Storm** (a spinning ring of metal
plates), cannot affect platinum (make a "refined armour" counter item). Sand - **Dune Sail** (sand-surf
movement), **Sandspout** (sand tornado), **Sandblind**. Lava (rare) - **Magma Flow** (lava river that cools into
rock), **Molten Rise** (lava pillars). Seismic - **Tremor Sense** (reveal hidden players through the ground;
fails on airborne/sand), **Echo Pulse**.

## FIRE
Ember Fist: **Flame Jab** strings, **Arc Kick** (crescent of fire), **Heat Palm** (point-blank blast). Blaze:
**Flare Bolt** (fire blast), **Fire Whip**, **Wildfire Wall**, **Ultimate: Inferno Pillar**. Rocket: **Jet Dash**,
**Comet Kick** (jet-propelled dive kick), **Flare Hover**.
Sub-skills: Lightning - **Arc Shot** (needs a stand-still channel: lore "calm mind"; interrupted if hit),
**Storm Fork** (ultimate). Redirection - **Return Current** (parry incoming lightning and fire it back; through
the stomach animation). Combustion (rare) - **Mind Flare** (forehead blast, explodes on impact; if interrupted
mid-cast it backfires). Dragon fire (legendary) - **True Flame** (rainbow-colour fire breath), **Dragon Dance**
(a two-player synced form if both have it).

## AIR
Gale: **Air Palm** (blast), **Wind Crescent** (thrown air blade), **Updraft** (launch). Cyclone: **Vortex** (pull
and spin), **Twister** (moving tornado), **Ultimate: Tempest** (a huge cyclone). Drift: **Air Scooter** (movement),
**Glider** (movement), **Cushion** (negate fall/knockback).
Sub-skills: Flight (legendary) - **Unbound** (free flight for a time). Projection - **Spirit Walk** (scout as a
spirit; body vulnerable). Suffocation (Outlaw) - **Breathless** (a channelled choke; lore-dark).

## AVATAR
**Avatar State** (meter ultimate): glowing eyes, the orbiting four-element sphere, empowered versions of the
player's learned elements' moves; **Energy Lock** (energybending: strip bending for a time; legendary).

## NON-BENDERS
Chi-blocker: **Pressure Strikes** (stacks chi-block), **Limb Lock**. Weapon master: **Iron Dao** strings, **Fan
Gust** (non-bending fan swipes), **Return Blade** (boomerang). Engineer: **Shock Glove**, **Grapple Line**.
