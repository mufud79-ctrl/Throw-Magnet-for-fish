# Bending lore reference (what the game must stay accurate to)

Source canon: the two animated series (the original series and its sequel set ~70 years later) plus the comics
and novels where noted. When a fact below is marked *(sequel)*, *(comics)* or *(novels)* it comes from that
material, not the first series. If you're unsure whether something is canon, say so and don't invent it.

## Core rules of the world
- **One element per person.** A bender is born able to bend exactly one element (or none). Only the **Avatar**
  bends all four. Nobody else ever learns a second element. Skill trees must not give players two elements.
- **Bending is learned through a martial art** and a philosophy. Power grows with discipline, not just stats.
- **Each element was learned from an original source:** water from the Moon (push and pull of the tides),
  earth from the badgermoles (they bend to tunnel; neutral jing, listening), fire from the dragons (fire as life
  and energy, breath-driven), air from the sky bison.
- **Chi.** Bending flows through chi paths; chi-blocking (pressure-point strikes) temporarily disables a bender's
  bending and limbs. Energybending can take bending away permanently.
- **Spirits and the Spirit World** are real. Benders can be possessed, spirits can be calmed, and some sub-skills
  are spiritual (projection, spirit purification).

## Celestial and environmental cycles (strong game events, all canon)
| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| Daytime / sun | Firebending stronger (fire draws on the sun). |
| Night | Waterbending stronger (the Moon). |
| **Full moon** | Waterbending at its peak; classic bloodbending is only possible then. |
| **Lunar eclipse** | Waterbenders lose their bending until it passes. |
| **Solar eclipse** | Firebenders lose their bending until it passes. |
| **The comet** (a comet that returns every ~100 years) | Firebending hugely amplified while it passes. |
| **Harmonic convergence** (every ~10,000 years) *(sequel)* | The spirit portals' power peaks; the world is reshaped. |
| Earthbenders on the ground | Strongest; weak in the air, on ships, or anywhere without earth to bend. |
| Water sources | Waterbenders need water nearby: oceans, snow, water skins, plants, the air's moisture (masters can pull water out of plants or the air; deserts are a real weakness). |
| Airbenders in open space / air | Strongest when mobile; defensive, evasive style. |

## WATER (fighting style: Tai Chi; "push and pull", adaptability, redirection)
Core skills: water whips, streams, waves, water spouts, ice (freezing, ice daggers, ice walls/prisons), snow,
steam and mist (heating isn't waterbending, but moving steam/mist/fog is), pulling water out of the air or plants
(masters), the "octopus" multi-tendril form, riding waves / ice-surfing.
Sub-skills:
- **Healing.** Water glows as it's guided along chi paths to heal wounds and restore energy. Mostly a gift taught
  by healers; spirit water from a sacred oasis is far stronger. It can't fix everything (deep spiritual or
  poison damage can need other means).
- **Plantbending.** Bending the water inside plants: vines, roots, giant swamp-plant bodies, kelp. Developed by a
  swamp community. Works only where there are plants.
- **Bloodbending.** Controlling the water in a living body. **Classic form needs a full moon.** A handful of very
  rare benders could do it without the moon by **psychic bloodbending** *(sequel)*. It's considered evil and is
  **outlawed** after its abuse. In the game: a forbidden, criminal path (bounty, wanted status), full-moon-gated.
- **Spirit purification / spiritbending** *(sequel)*. Waterbenders from the polar north can calm or purify dark
  spirits and bend spirit energy. Spiritual, not combat-first.
- **Ice and snow mastery** is core, not a sub-skill, but a style can specialise in it (northern masters).
- (Not canon: "lava waterbending", "cloud bending" as a sub-skill. Don't add them.)

## EARTH (fighting style: Hung Gar; for the seismic master, Southern Praying Mantis; neutral jing: wait, listen, strike)
Core skills: rock throws, pillars/columns, walls, trenches, earth waves and surfing on moving earth, tunnelling,
stone armour/gauntlets, quicksand, mud (with water present), boulders, sinking enemies into the ground.
Sub-skills:
- **Metalbending.** Invented by a blind master who could feel the earth particles still inside metal. Bends
  metal with impurities; **cannot bend refined metals like platinum.** Later a whole police force uses it with
  metal cables *(sequel)*.
- **Seismic sense.** "Seeing" through vibrations in the ground (barefoot). Lets the bender sense people and
  objects; blind spots: sand (fuzzy), the air (anyone off the ground), wooden floors (weaker).
- **Sandbending.** Desert tribes bend sand: sand sailing (sand-sailers), sand clouds, sandstorms/tornadoes,
  sand blades, burying. Sand is finer and less forceful than rock.
- **Lavabending.** Extremely rare: turning earth to lava and cooling it back. A few known benders across eras
  *(sequel, novels)*. In the game: a rare innate gift, never a common pick.
- **Glass/crystal bending**: not canon. Don't add it.

## FIRE (fighting style: Northern Shaolin; breath is the source; fire as life and energy, not only rage)
Core skills: fire blasts and fists, fire whips, fire daggers/blades, kicks, fire shields and walls, jet
propulsion (rocketing with fire from the feet/hands), breath of fire, heat control (warming the body or a cup).
Sub-skills:
- **Lightning generation.** "Cold-blooded fire": the bender separates positive and negative energy and lets it
  reunite as lightning. Requires calm and peace of mind; hard to master.
- **Lightning redirection.** A defensive technique derived from studying waterbenders: catch the lightning, lead
  it through the arms and down through the stomach (never the heart), and send it back out.
- **Combustionbending.** Extremely rare: focusing energy through the forehead (marked with a third-eye tattoo)
  and firing it as an explosive blast. Disrupting their breathing/focus can make it backfire.
- **Dragon fire ("true fire").** Learned from dragons at an ancient sun-warrior city: the original, purest fire,
  seen in its "true colours" (a rainbow of colour). Also the Dancing Dragon form. A legendary, spiritual path.
- **Sun-warrior "eternal flame"** and heat sensing are lore flavour, not fighting sub-skills.
- (A certain character's **blue fire** is personal to that character, not a sub-element: don't make it a
  playable sub-skill. If you want flame colour as a reward, make it cosmetic and generic.)

## AIR (fighting style: Baguazhang; circling, evasion, never meet force head-on)
Core skills: air blasts, air swipes/blades, air shields and spheres, tornadoes and vortexes, air cushions,
enhanced speed and jumps, gliders (staff glider), the **air scooter** (a ball of air to ride), riding
wind currents.
Sub-skills:
- **Flight.** Unaided flight. Requires total detachment from all earthly attachments. Only legendary gurus and
  one sequel antagonist achieve it. In the game: an endgame enlightenment quest, very rare.
- **Spiritual projection** *(sequel)*. Projecting one's spirit across distance (astral projection). A gift of
  very spiritual airbenders. Utility/scouting, not damage.
- **Suffocation / removing air from lungs** *(sequel antagonist)*: a dark, murderous technique. If used at all,
  it's an outlaw-path move, not standard.

## AVATAR-ONLY
- **The Avatar** is one person at a time who reincarnates in a fixed cycle: **Fire -> Air -> Water -> Earth**
  (then Fire again). The new Avatar is born in the next element's nation and learns the others in cycle order
  from their native element. If the Avatar is killed while in the Avatar State, the cycle ends forever.
- **Avatar State.** A defence mechanism and a link to all past Avatars: glowing eyes (and markings on an
  airbender Avatar), massive power, multiple elements at once (the iconic orbiting sphere of all four elements).
- **Energybending.** Taught by the ancient lion turtles: bending the energy within oneself and others; can remove
  or restore bending. Avatar-only (or lion turtles).

## Non-benders (lore paths so non-bending players have a real role)
- **Chi-blocker**: pressure-point strikes that disable bending and limbs temporarily *(both series)*.
- **Swordsmen and weapon masters**: swords, fans, dual broadswords, boomerangs, bows.
- **Inventors / engineers** *(sequel era)*: electrified gloves, mecha, glider suits, cars and airships.
- Elite warrior orders (fan-and-makeup warriors, archers, etc.) exist in lore; make your own orders rather than
  copying a named one.

## Combinations that exist in lore (useful for co-op combos between two players)
- Water + fire -> steam/fog clouds. Earth + water -> mud. Air + earth -> dust storms. Air + fire -> larger
  flames / firestorms. Earth + fire -> lava is a *single* rare earthbender, not two players combining.

## IP note
The setting is someone else's IP. Using the *mechanics* (one element, sub-skills, cycles, the Avatar concept) is
common in fan games, but a Roblox game can still be taken down. Keep **show titles, character names, logos,
nation insignias and named techniques** out of the shipped game; name your own nations, styles, characters and
moves. Use generic descriptive terms (waterbending, metalbending, lightning) and original move names.
