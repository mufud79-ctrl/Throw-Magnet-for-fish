# Game systems (brainstorm, lore-accurate): how four elements become a deep roster

The problem: "just the 4 elements would be super lame to play as." The fix, without breaking lore: **one element per
player, but many ways to bend it.** Depth comes from (1) sub-skills, (2) fighting styles inside each element, (3) the
environment and the sky changing what you can do, (4) non-bender paths, and (5) one Avatar per server.

## 1. Build = Element + Style + Sub-skill(s) + Path
- **Element** (rolled or picked at character creation): Water, Earth, Fire, Air, or Non-bender.
- **Style** (picked at a teacher, swappable later for a cost): a school of the element with its own move set. Styles
  are original names for lore-true ways of fighting:
  - Water: *Tide* (whips, waves, redirection), *Glacier* (ice: daggers, walls, prisons), *Mist* (steam/fog, evasive).
  - Earth: *Bedrock* (pillars, boulders, walls), *Stonehide* (armour, gauntlets, grappling), *Tremor* (waves, sinking,
    seismic play).
  - Fire: *Ember Fist* (close-range kicks and punches), *Blaze* (long-range blasts, whips), *Rocket* (jet-propelled
    mobility).
  - Air: *Gale* (blasts, crescents), *Cyclone* (vortexes, tornadoes, crowd control), *Drift* (scooter, glider, evasion).
- **Sub-skills** (unlocked, see section 2): each element has 3-5. A player equips **one major sub-skill** (its own
  move slot set) and can learn others over time. Sub-skills never cross elements.
- **Path** (morality/role, affects NPCs and access): Healer, Guardian, Mercenary, Outlaw, Spiritualist. Outlaw is how
  forbidden techniques (bloodbending, suffocation) fit lore: you can learn them, but you become wanted.

## 2. Sub-skill unlocks (rarity that matches lore)
| Element | Learned (quest + training) | Rare gift (innate roll, low %) | Legendary (endgame) | Forbidden (Outlaw path) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Water | Healing (needs the gift, ~common among water players), Plantbending (swamp quest), Ice mastery | Spirit purification (polar-north spiritual gift) | Spirit-water healing (sacred oasis) | Bloodbending (full moon only); Psychic bloodbending (legendary-rare, no moon) |
| Earth | Metalbending (blind-master school quest), Sandbending (desert tribe), Seismic sense (barefoot training) | Lavabending (very rare) | Metal cable mastery, seismic "true sight" | - |
| Fire | Lightning generation (calm-mind trials), Lightning redirection (taught by a firebending master; derived from waterbending) | Combustionbending (very rare, third-eye) | Dragon fire / true colours (sun-warrior ruins + a dragon trial) | - |
| Air | Advanced gliding / scooter mastery | Spiritual projection | Flight (detachment trial: let go of gear/attachments) | Suffocation (dark technique) |
- A **gift roll** at character creation (rerollable with an item or by rebirth) decides innate rare gifts. Keep the
  odds low (lava, combustion ~1-2 %, projection ~3 %). Learned sub-skills must be reachable by everyone through play.
- **Mastery levels** per sub-skill (Novice -> Adept -> Master) unlock moves and passives, e.g. metal: plates ->
  cables -> bending metal armour of enemies; healing: self -> others -> revive downed allies.

## 3. Moves per build (keeps fights varied)
- Slots: **M1 combo** (style basic strings), **Z X C V** (style moves), **B** (sub-skill move), **F** (style ultimate or
  sub-skill ultimate at Master). Movement tech per element (water: wave-ride / ice-slide; earth: earth-surf, pillar
  launch; fire: jet dash; air: scooter / glider / triple jump).
- Every move needs a source rule: water needs a water source or pulls it from the air (slower), earth needs ground
  contact (no casting mid-air except carried rocks), fire is weaker at night and stronger at day, air is strongest
  when moving.

## 4. The world fights back (lore cycles as live events)
- **Day/night cycle**: fire +damage by day, water +damage at night.
- **Full moon night** (server event): water power peak; bloodbending enabled for Outlaws.
- **Lunar eclipse** (rare event, ~3 min): all waterbenders lose bending; non-benders and chi-blockers shine.
- **Solar eclipse** (rare event): firebenders lose bending.
- **The comet** (very rare, scheduled big event): firebending massively amplified for a set time.
- **Terrain**: deserts (water weak, sand strong), swamps (plants), snowfields (water/ice strong), ships/bridges (earth
  weak), mountains/temples (air strong), metal cities (metalbenders shine).

## 5. The Avatar (one per server, lore-true)
- Only **one Avatar** exists. When the Avatar dies normally, the next Avatar is a new player chosen from the **next
  element in the cycle Fire -> Air -> Water -> Earth** (opt-in candidates with high mastery, or a weighted roll).
- The Avatar starts with their native element and unlocks the others in cycle order by finding teachers
  (quests), with the **Avatar State** as a meter-based ultimate (glowing eyes, the orbiting four-element sphere,
  multi-element moves). **Energybending** is the Avatar's legendary unlock (lion-turtle quest): it can strip an
  Outlaw's bending for a time.
- **Killed in the Avatar State = the cycle ends** for that server (no Avatar until the next server/season reset):
  huge risk, huge drama. Keep it as a seasonal mechanic.

## 6. Non-bender roles (so "no element" is a real choice)
- **Chi-blocker**: hits stack chi-block; at full stack the target loses bending for a few seconds.
- **Weapon master**: swords, fans, boomerang (returns), bow; parry windows.
- **Engineer** (later era): electrified gloves, grappling cables, glider suit, gadgets.
- Non-benders get the strongest eclipse-event bonuses.

## 7. Co-op element combos (two players, lore-true)
Water + fire = steam cloud (vision block), earth + water = mud field (slow), air + earth = dust storm (blind),
air + fire = firestorm (bigger fire). Trigger when two players' moves overlap in a short window.

## 8. Naming rule (IP)
Use original names for nations, styles, sub-skill quests, teachers, places and every move. Keep the lore *rules*.
