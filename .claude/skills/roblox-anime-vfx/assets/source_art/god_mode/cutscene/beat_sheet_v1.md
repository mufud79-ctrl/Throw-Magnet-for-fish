# Raijin Ascension: transformation cutscene, pitch v1

Storyboard: `storyboard_v1.jpg` (frames `shot1..7.png`, Figma image gen). Total ~3.4 s, the caster is invulnerable throughout.
The idea: the storm *notices* you. A giant eye opens in the sky, two heartbeats sync to the drums that are about to exist,
the strike doesn't stop (it pours), the player's silhouette is swapped for the god's inside the beam, the drums are forged
from 8 lightning strikes, and the god's first act is a single drum beat that scorches a full circle of Lichtenberg scars.

## Roblox side
- Scripted camera (`CameraType.Scriptable`) driven by the spec's camera keys (pos / look / fov), letterbox as a ScreenGui, grade as ColorCorrection keys.
- Only the caster sees the full cinematic camera; other players see the world VFX (eye, strike, beam, forge, shockwave) from their own camera.
- The model swap happens inside the white beam (beat 4), so the cut from avatar to god model is hidden.
- New previewer/runtime features needed: volumetric mesh beam (also being added in the moves sub-session), slow-motion time scale on effects + animations, silhouette (pure black) mode, decal grow for the scar ring.

## Changes from the first draft
- The draft's cocoon and glass-crack beat is replaced by the **pour + silhouette swap**, which reads more clearly and hides the model swap.
- New: **sky eye** (the storm as a character), **heartbeats** that preview the drum rhythm, **drum forge** (the drums are born from lightning, not snapped into place), **first beat** scorching a full-circle scar (the fruit's calling card).
- Kept: the low-angle opening, the inverted impact frame on the strike, slow-mo reveal, drums locking in one by one, cyan grade at the end.
