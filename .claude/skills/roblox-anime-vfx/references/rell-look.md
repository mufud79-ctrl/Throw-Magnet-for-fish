# The RELL Seas look

Source: frames from RELL Games' official movies (The Movie, Movie 2, Movie 3, "Light Upon the Seas", "Into The
Seas") and ~15 gameplay showcase videos (fruit showcases, finishers, haki), pulled from YouTube's published
thumbnail and 25/50/75% auto-frames (YouTube blocks headless playback with a CAPTCHA; don't try to bypass it).

## Scene
Bright daylight arenas: saturated blue sky, sand or stone ground, blocky Roblox characters. Effects must read
against a *bright* background, so they rely on saturated colour and dark accents (smoke, crust, black cores), not
on darkness around them. Heavy bloom on hot cores.

## Impacts
White god-ray streaks fanning out, white anime dust clouds with soft shading, blocky debris thrown, sharp
multi-spike star flash on close hits, damage numbers.

## Players' criticism to avoid
Early RELL fruit VFX were called "mediocre"; the old Gura was "light blue squiggly lines". Squiggles, flat
outlines and thin generic particles are exactly what the bar rules out.
